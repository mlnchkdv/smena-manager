Privacy policy

1. Data we process

smena.team processes approved members' names, verified email addresses, roles and teams; workspace projects, tasks, comments, knowledge, bookmarks, files, settings and activity; signed session and synchronization data; browser push subscriptions; and each member's notification preferences.

2. Google user data

Google Sign-In supplies an identifier, name and verified email address to match a person to an approved profile. Workspace data and team uploads are stored in the workspace owner's Google Drive account.

For email notifications, smena.team requests only https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send. It is used to send assignment, comment, mention, deadline and optional digest notifications selected by users. The application does not read, change or delete existing email, contacts or Gmail settings.

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Purpose, storage and security

Data is used for authentication, team collaboration, file synchronization, selected notifications, security, backup and recovery. Workspace content is stored in a dedicated Drive folder. OAuth refresh tokens and delivery keys are server-side secrets and are never sent to the browser. Sessions are signed, updates are versioned, and roles are enforced by the server.

4. Sharing

We do not sell user data or use it for advertising. Data is shared only with infrastructure providers needed for these functions: Google for identity, Drive and Gmail; Aeza for hosting; and the push service of the selected browser. Other team members' access is limited by workspace membership and role.

5. Retention, control and contact

Workspace data is retained while the workspace is in use or until an administrator deletes it. Users may disable email and push notifications, revoke Google access in their Google Account, or request profile and related data deletion by contacting hello@smena.agency. Material changes to Google data use will be disclosed before the new use begins.
